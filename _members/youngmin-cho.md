Young Min (Jeffrey) Cho received his PhD in Computer and Information Science from the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Lyle Ungar and Sharath Guntuku. His research focused on applications of large language models, with a particular interest in developing conversational AI systems to support mental health. He is now an Applied Scientist at Amazon.
